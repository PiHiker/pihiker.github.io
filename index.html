PiHiker / Engineering field notes

Privacy, security, and Bitcoin.

In penetration testing, one of my first questions is “What can someone on the other side of this connection learn?” That question also shapes how I build: send mining traffic over Tor, make API calls without an account, and give administrators a way to see who holds privileged access.

Field note / Experimental P2Poolv2 work

What should a node do when Tor goes offline?

In onion-only mode, losing connectivity is the right outcome. A direct connection might keep shares flowing, but it would expose the network address the operator chose to keep private.

My experimental patches for P2Poolv2 address two separate questions: how to keep peer connections on Tor, and how to discover another peer by its onion address.
